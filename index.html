Broccoli Topic maps for search results

Read a search as a map of its topics

Pick a source above and search the way you would there. Broccoli fetches up to 2,000 results and groups them into labelled topics, so you can see what a literature is made of before you read it. Click a topic to zoom in, and it splits into subtopics of its own.
